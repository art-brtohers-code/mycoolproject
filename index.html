The field guide / No. 01

A few
good turns.

  1. Follow your appetite. Use the arrow keys, WASD, or swipe the field. Each crop makes you longer.
  2. Fill the ticket. Marked crops count toward your order. A full basket earns 10 extra points.
  3. Find a rhythm. Pick crops in quick succession to build a multiplier, up to ×5. A golden beet is worth 5 extra points.
  4. Leave yourself room. Avoid the edges, your tail, and stones. An outlined warning appears before a new stone settles.
  1. Take the night shift. Use arrows, WASD, swipes, or direction buttons. You move continuously. Request a turn before the junction and it happens as soon as the path opens.
  2. Clear your plate. Eat every pellet to clear the maze, earn 500 bonus points, and begin a faster night in a new ink palette. Small pellets earn 10 points; flower lanterns earn 50.
  3. Turn the chase around. Lanterns temporarily make ghosts edible. Catch them for 200, 400, 800, then 1,600 points. Their eyes return to the glasshouse.
  4. Make yourself scarce. The two side paths connect. You have three lives; a ghost costs one. Earn an extra life every 10,000 points. Fruit appears twice each night and lasts 12 seconds.

Daily plots reset at midnight UTC. Everyone gets the same seed and rules; your choices shape the run. Practice has a slower pace, no stones, and no records.

Space or P pauses. M toggles sound. Switching tabs pauses the game automatically.

Switch between Snake and Pac-Man above the game. Your other run stays paused until you return and resume it. Each game keeps its own personal best.

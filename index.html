How to play

  1. Use the arrow keys, WASD, or swipe to move. Food makes the snake longer.
  2. Collect the outlined food to fill an order and earn 10 extra points.
  3. Collect food quickly to build a multiplier up to x5. Gold food gives 5 extra points.
  4. Avoid walls, your tail, and rocks. A red outline shows where a new rock will appear.
  1. Use the arrow keys, WASD, or swipe. You can request a turn before reaching a corner.
  2. Eat all the dots to clear the maze and earn 500 extra points. Small dots give 10 points; big dots give 50.
  3. Big dots turn ghosts blue for a short time. Catch blue ghosts for 200, 400, 800, then 1,600 points.
  4. You start with three lives. The side tunnels connect. Every 10,000 points earns an extra life.
